# BHG Idea System Foundation

## Purpose

Define a preliminary institutional model for capturing, preserving, evaluating, evolving, implementing, and archiving ideas within BHG.

This is a **Draft architectural proposal**. It is not constitutional canon and does not authorize changes to BHG governance.

## Core principle

A valuable idea must remain recoverable independently of the person, conversation, tool, or AI model that originated it.

Each idea should be capable of acquiring identity, context, provenance, lifecycle state, maturity, evidence, dependencies, decisions, implementation references, and history.

## Four independent dimensions

1. **Lifecycle** — current process state.
2. **Maturity** — institutional confidence and authority earned.
3. **Gates** — evidence and verification required to advance.
4. **Evidence** — auditable basis for decisions and promotion.

One dimension must never be inferred solely from another.

## Maturity model

- **L4 — Experimental:** hypothesis, exploration, prototype, or unvalidated concept.
- **L3 — Operational:** demonstrated and usable within defined boundaries.
- **L2 — Architectural:** validated enough to become a governed architectural standard.
- **L1 — Constitutional:** exceptionally stable principle or constraint suitable for canonical authority.

L1 is intentionally difficult to reach.

## Stability and innovation

L1/L2 protect institutional stability. L3 enables controlled operational adoption. L4 provides an explicit experimental environment.

Experimental success does not automatically create institutional authority.

## Promotion principle

> The higher the requested authority, the stronger the evidence, verification, compatibility, continuity, and risk assessment required.

Time in existence does not itself grant authority.

## Experimental Governance

BHG may maintain an explicit experimental space where uncertain but potentially valuable ideas can be explored without contaminating stable institutional layers.

Experiments should record scope, owner, hypothesis, success criteria, risk boundary, evidence requirements, review date, and promotion or termination criteria.

## Reversibility

Promotion does not automatically mean irreversibility. Higher-level elements remain subject to applicable review, supersession, controlled deprecation, or exceptional constitutional review.

## Relationship to Genesis

This framework is intended to reconcile the historical Genesis, idea intake, knowledge capture, innovation pipeline, and corporate memory concepts with the present BHG-Knowledge architecture.

Implementation remains subject to architectural reconciliation before automation.

## Current status

This document is intentionally **Draft**. It must be reconciled with BHG-Governance, existing BHG-Knowledge standards, historical Genesis specifications, evidence standards, and future automation requirements.
