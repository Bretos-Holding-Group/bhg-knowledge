# IDEA-2026-0003

## Concept

Evolve BHG-Knowledge toward a machine-readable institutional memory architecture in which important knowledge objects preserve identity, authority, provenance, evidence, relationships, version history, and decision rationale.

## Potential future capability

An institutional knowledge object could answer:

- why it exists;
- who or what originated it;
- which evidence supports it;
- which decisions depend on it;
- what superseded it;
- which entities are governed by it;
- when it should be reviewed.

## Constraints

This is an L4 research hypothesis. It does not mandate a specific database, AI platform, graph technology, or implementation architecture.
