# BHG Institutional Idea Registry

This directory is the canonical capture area for BHG ideas until an idea becomes an approved project, governed standard, or another formally governed artifact.

## Rules

1. Every institutional idea receives an immutable `idea_id`.
2. Ideas are preserved even when rejected, deferred, superseded, or archived.
3. Lifecycle state and maturity are recorded independently.
4. Evidence and decision rationale are referenced explicitly.
5. Promotion requires the applicable gate.
6. This registry does not itself grant governance authority.
7. New automation must validate records rather than silently modify their meaning.
