## Interpretation rules

- Entries are not approvals.
- `L4` is the safe default for recovered historical concepts.
- Historical recurrence does not increase maturity.
- Related entries may later be merged or decomposed.
- Ziva-specific concepts are retained for institutional provenance but must not automatically become BHG-level initiatives.
- Exact source timestamps and original wording remain subject to further historical recovery.

## Recovery limitations

This register is based on historical context currently available to the recovery process. It is intentionally explicit about uncertainty and should be extended when additional historical source material becomes accessible.
