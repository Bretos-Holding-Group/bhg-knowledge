# IDEA-2026-0001

## Concept

Establish a four-level institutional maturity model (L4 experimental, L3 operational, L2 architectural, L1 constitutional) combined with lifecycle states, evidence requirements, and increasingly strict promotion gates.

## Key insight

BHG should not have to choose between conservatism and aggressive innovation. Stable layers can protect institutional continuity while experimentation occurs in a bounded environment.

## Potential outcomes

- Idea Registry
- Experimental Governance
- Promotion Gates
- Evidence-backed certification
- Future automation for lifecycle verification
- Controlled promotion toward architectural and constitutional authority

## Constraints

This idea is not constitutional. It must be reconciled with existing BHG governance and historical Genesis specifications before implementation becomes mandatory.

## Evidence required for future promotion

- architecture reconciliation;
- governance compatibility review;
- lifecycle validation;
- gate criteria validation;
- metadata validation;
- automation feasibility assessment;
- independent verification appropriate to the requested maturity level.
