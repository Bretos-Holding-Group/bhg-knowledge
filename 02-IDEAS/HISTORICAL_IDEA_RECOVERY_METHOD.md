# Historical Idea Recovery Method

## Purpose

Establish a controlled method for recovering ideas and related concepts that originated in prior BHG conversations, working sessions, research, or project development and may not yet exist in the institutional knowledge repository.

The purpose is **preservation and classification, not approval**.

## Core rule

> Recovering an idea does not authorize its implementation, adoption, promotion, or canonization.

Recovered material enters the system at the lowest defensible maturity unless existing evidence demonstrates otherwise.

## Recovery sequence

1. Identify historical source material available to the recovery process.
2. Extract candidate concepts without prematurely rewriting their meaning.
3. Preserve provenance and approximate origin context.
4. Deduplicate related candidates.
5. Distinguish ideas from principles, hypotheses, standards, architectures, systems, products, projects, features, research, decisions, and assets.
6. Establish relationships such as parent, derivative, related, merged, superseded, or duplicate.
7. Assign lifecycle state independently from maturity.
8. Record evidence and unresolved uncertainty.
9. Register the candidate in the institutional idea system.
10. Require the applicable promotion Gate before increasing authority.

## Historical provenance

Every recovered candidate should preserve, where available:

- source context;
- approximate origin date;
- original terminology;
- evolution notes;
- related discussions or projects;
- evidence already produced;
- known decisions affecting the idea.

When exact historical provenance cannot be established, uncertainty must be recorded rather than inferred as fact.

## Non-canonization safeguard

Historical frequency, founder enthusiasm, age, repetition, or apparent strategic importance are not sufficient grounds for promotion to L2 or L1.

## Recovery status vocabulary

- `RECOVERY_CANDIDATE`
- `RECOVERED`
- `NORMALIZED`
- `DUPLICATE`
- `MERGED`
- `DERIVED`
- `SUPERSEDED`
- `UNRESOLVED`

These statuses describe recovery/classification work and do not replace the normal idea lifecycle.

## Relationship to Genesis

This method is compatible with the historical Genesis concept of capturing, classifying, relating, evaluating, and transforming knowledge. Genesis is treated as a processing capability; the institutional repository remains the durable record of the resulting knowledge objects.
