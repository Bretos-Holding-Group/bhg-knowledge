# IDEA-2026-0002

## Concept

Develop a multi-level BHG corporate taxonomy covering at least entity type, business sector, industry, sub-industry, ecosystem role, and asset relationships.

## Initial hypothesis

BHG should be classified primarily as a diversified holding/group rather than as a technology or fintech company. Operating entities such as Ziva Latam should retain their own industry classifications while remaining structurally related to the holding.

## Future research

- jurisdiction-specific legal classifications;
- accounting and reporting classifications;
- investor and banking classifications;
- industry taxonomies;
- asset taxonomy;
- entity relationship model;
- versioning and migration rules.

## Constraints

This is an L4 research hypothesis. It does not establish a legal classification or constitutional standard.
