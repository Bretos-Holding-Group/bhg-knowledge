# Quick Idea Capture Protocol

## Purpose

Provide a low-friction mechanism for capturing ideas before analysis, prioritization, or implementation.

The protocol exists to prevent loss of potentially valuable concepts when the author cannot immediately develop them.

## Principle

> **Capture first. Evaluate later.**

An idea does not need to be complete, coherent, feasible, or strategically aligned to be preserved.

## Minimum capture

A valid quick capture requires only:

1. a temporary or permanent identifier;
2. the idea in the author's own words, or the closest recoverable wording;
3. capture date/context when available;
4. optional tags;
5. optional links to related ideas.

Everything else may remain unknown.

## Deferred enrichment

After capture, the system may progressively add:

- problem statement;
- proposed solution;
- category;
- object class;
- entity ownership;
- relationships;
- evidence;
- risks;
- lifecycle state;
- maturity;
- Gate requirements;
- implementation references.

Incomplete ideas must not be rejected solely because their metadata is incomplete.

## Recovery and reactivation

A previously captured idea may be reopened years later. Reactivation should preserve the original record and append a new development event rather than overwriting historical provenance.

## Anti-friction rule

The capture mechanism should not require the author to decide immediately whether an idea is a project, product, standard, business, or constitutional principle.

Classification can be deferred.

## Draft/L4 boundary

This protocol is a Draft/L4 proposal. It does not authorize implementation of captured ideas and does not establish constitutional authority.
